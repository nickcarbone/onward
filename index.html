Settings & data sources

Keys are stored only in this browser (localStorage) and sent only to the provider they belong to.

UK National Rail (optional)

UK boards already run live through the community Huxley2 proxy. A free Rail Data Marketplace key (subscribe to “Live Departure Board”) connects directly to Darwin instead.

Airports

Airport boards need one key. AeroDataBox (via RapidAPI) has an instant free tier and the most board-like data. Without a key the airport boards show a service notice.

About the data

Every departure shown comes from a published source; nothing is generated. Live sources: National Rail Darwin, SBB (transport.opendata.ch), Amtrak (Amtraker), and operator real-time feeds carried by Transitous (NS, LIRR, parts of DB). Everywhere else the board shows the operator’s published timetable and the corner badge says TIMETABLE.

Timetables: Transitous and its sources (map data © OpenStreetMap contributors). UK live data: National Rail Enquiries / Rail Delivery Group, via Huxley2. Swiss data: transport.opendata.ch. Amtrak: Amtraker. Chimes are original synthesis.
